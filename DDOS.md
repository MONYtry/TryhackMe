# ℹ️ What does DDoS stand for?
Distributed-Denial-of-Service Attack can take down Websites, Servers or anything with Internet-Connection but is based on the power of it. 

# ⚙️ Usecase of DDoS-Attacks?
Lets say a hacker wants to shut down a hole company, instead of walking inside of the building and breaking the servers he would just do a DDoS-Attack <br>
Also it is often used to get attention on one server while the hacker is walking in the background of a different server from the company<br> 

# 💥 DDoS but simple
Lets say a hacker wants to shut down a hole company, instead of walking inside of the building and breaking the servers he would just do: <br>
- Get the companys IP-Adress by just pinging the domain and then use NMAP to find the right port.
- Then create a simple Botnet Script and get some compremised machines.
- After that he (Botmaster) sends out commands as: /start UDP (ip) (packet size) 
- Every connected user will be sending out data without even noticing anything.
Shortly after that the company might have problems with keeping up by a lot of traffic, this is called UDP-DDoS-Flooding.


# 💥 DoS but simple
Same senario as before, but here is a big problem. Lets say the hacker makes a simple python file that connects to the server and sends out UDP-Packets.
It won't do ANYTHING, because one WiFi could never win a fight against an big server with good specs, UNLESS you have a BIIIGGGG Computer and extremely good WiFi, because when you send out packages your UPLOAD Rate will be very bad.
So you basically bottelneck yourself via. Upload-Speed
